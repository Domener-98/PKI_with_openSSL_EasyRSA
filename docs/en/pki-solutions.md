# Overview of PKI solutions on the market

[← Back to README](../../README.md)

## 1. Microsoft: Active Directory Certificate Services (AD CS)

Microsoft offers a PKI built into its ecosystem through **AD CS**. It allows an internal PKI to be deployed within an Active Directory domain and simplifies identity and certificate management in Windows environments.

AD CS handles Certificate Revocation Lists (CRL), can be paired with an OCSP service and provides automation features such as certificate auto-enrollment, which reduces the administrative workload.

## 2. Dedicated commercial offerings

These offerings provide complete, professional PKI solutions for organisations with high requirements in terms of security, reliability and regulatory compliance.

**CyberTrust Consulting.** A PKI consultancy that supports companies with strategy, modernisation and compliance. Its four main services: auditing an existing PKI architecture with a tailored roadmap, designing CA hierarchies (separation of roles, lifecycles, assurance levels), automating the certificate lifecycle and ensuring compliance with international standards.

**Entrust.** A major player in digital trust, offering full certificate lifecycle management as well as authentication and identity protection services, mainly for large organisations.

## 3. Open source solutions

They make it possible to deploy a PKI with no licensing cost and a high degree of flexibility.

**OpenPKI.** A complete solution for managing certificates and their lifecycle, suited to environments with specialised technical skills.

**IdealX.** Solutions positioned between open source and commercial: easier to deploy than some full open source PKIs, while remaining flexible.

## 4. Outsourced solutions (Certplus, Verisign, etc.)

Certificate management is entrusted to a trusted third-party provider. This approach is common for public certificates, especially for websites. It simplifies management and offers a high level of trust, but creates a dependency on the provider and a recurring cost.

## 5. Selection criteria

Choosing a PKI depends on the size of the organisation, the budget, security needs, in-house skills and regulatory constraints.

In practice, open source solutions are often used for educational or technical purposes, commercial or integrated solutions are preferred in companies, and outsourced solutions are chosen when ease of management and public trust are the priorities.
